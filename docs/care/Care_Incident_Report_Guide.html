Incident Report Guide

Effective Date: 1 March 2026 | Version: 2.0 | Sample RAG Knowledge Base
Document ID: CARE-IR-002 | Category: Care

All accidents, incidents, near misses, medication errors, safeguarding concerns, and property or data breaches related to care delivery must be reported. This guide explains classification, immediate actions, documentation fields, sample narratives, and follow-up within HomeCare Scheduler operations.

2. Immediate Actions (Strict Order)

  1. Make safe - protect client and yourself from further harm.
  2. Call 112/999 if medical emergency; do not delay for office approval.
  3. First aid only within your training limits.
  4. Notify office / on-call supervisor by phone as soon as practical.
  5. Inform family / GP only as directed by office or care plan.
  6. Preserve scene where investigation may be needed; do not move equipment unnecessarily.
  7. Complete written report within 24 hours (same day preferred).

4. Sample Narratives

Near miss - dizziness during transfer

At 14:20 on 12 March 2026, during a sit-to-stand transfer from armchair to zimmer frame, Client Mary S. (ID C-1042) reported sudden dizziness. I supported her to sit back safely. No fall occurred. BP not taken (not authorised). I stayed with client, offered water, phoned office at 14:25. Office contacted daughter. Client remained alert. Visit continued with seated personal care only. Near miss logged; care plan review requested.

Medication prompting discrepancy

At 09:05 on 4 April 2026, during AM medication prompt, one tablet remained in Monday AM slot of blister pack though client stated she had not taken morning dose. I did not administer additional dose. I sealed pack, photographed slot per office instruction, and called roster office at 09:08. Supervisor advised to leave pack intact for pharmacist review and document refusal to double-dose. Client well, no adverse effect observed.

5. Follow-Up & Learning

Admin or clinical lead reviews within 2 working days. You may be invited to a debrief. Do not discuss the incident on social media. Anonymous learning points may appear in team briefings. Repeated similar incidents trigger care plan review and additional training.

7. Safeguarding Escalation Pathway

  1. Ensure immediate safety of client and others.
  2. Phone office / on-call supervisor.
  3. If immediate danger persists, contact An Garda Siochana (999) or HSE safeguarding.
  4. Complete written safeguarding report within 24 hours.
  5. Do not contact alleged perpetrator unless instructed by safeguarding lead.

9. FAQ

Do I report a near miss if nobody was hurt?

Yes. Near misses help prevent future accidents and are required within 24 hours.

What if the client asks me not to tell the office?

You must still report injuries, safeguarding concerns, and medication errors. Explain politely that reporting is mandatory.

Can I complete the report on paper?

Yes if app unavailable; hand to supervisor same day for entry into the system.

Urgent safeguarding: Do not wait for office callback if immediate danger - call Gardai / HSE safeguarding as per local pathway, then notify office.
