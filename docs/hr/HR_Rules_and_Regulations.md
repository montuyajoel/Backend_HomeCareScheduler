# Rules and Regulations

*Effective Date: 1 March 2026  |  Version: 2.0  |  Sample RAG Knowledge Base*

These Rules and Regulations apply to all HomeCare Scheduler employees, agency workers, and contractors who deliver or support home care services in the Republic of Ireland. Breaches may result in disciplinary action up to and including summary dismissal and may be reported to regulators (HIQA, HSE safeguarding pathways) where required by law.

![Conduct pillars](assets/conduct_pillars.png)

---

## 1. Purpose and Scope

This document sets minimum standards for professional conduct, confidentiality, attendance, safeguarding, medication boundaries, uniform, use of company systems, and disciplinary procedures. It supplements - but does not replace - statutory obligations, individual employment contracts, and client-specific care plans.

**Applies to:** caregivers, senior carers, roster staff, admin, and any person with access to client data or client homes.

---

## 2. Professional Conduct

| Area | Required behaviour | Not permitted |
|------|-------------------|---------------|
| Dignity & respect | Person-centred, courteous communication | Rudeness, discrimination, intimidation |
| Confidentiality | Protect client health, address, and personal data | Discussing clients on social media or with unauthorised persons |
| Gifts & boundaries | Declare small tokens; decline cash or high-value gifts | Accepting > EUR 20 gifts, loans, or personal favours |
| Professional distance | Warm but appropriate relationships | Borrowing/lending money with clients |
| Mobile phones | Care-related use; clock-in/out only when needed | Personal scrolling or calls during personal care |

Employees must not work under the influence of alcohol or non-prescribed drugs. Prescribed medication that may impair alertness must be declared to HR.

---

## 3. Timekeeping, Attendance & Roster Compliance

1. Arrive on time for every assigned shift. Notify the roster office immediately if you will be more than **10 minutes** late.
2. Clock in and clock out only from the **client's location** using the official HomeCare Scheduler app.
3. Falsifying time records, buddy clocking, or completing visits not performed is **gross misconduct**.
4. Do not leave a client early without completing required tasks and notifying the office.
5. Do not swap or cover shifts privately without office approval and system update.
6. Repeated lateness or unexplained no-shows trigger formal review.

| Attendance issue | First occurrence | Repeated (3 in 90 days) |
|-----------------|------------------|-------------------------|
| Late arrival (<30 min) | Verbal reminder | Written warning |
| No-show without call | Written warning | Final warning / dismissal review |
| Falsified clock record | Investigation | Summary dismissal if proven |

---

## 4. Safeguarding & Safety

Report any suspicion of abuse, neglect, self-neglect, or financial exploitation **immediately** to your supervisor and follow the Safeguarding Vulnerable Adults procedure. Do not investigate independently.

- Follow infection prevention: hand hygiene, PPE as directed, stay home when infectious.
- Never attempt a lift or transfer beyond your training or the care plan.
- Use provided equipment; do not improvise with unsafe substitutes.
- Report all incidents, near misses, and accidents within **24 hours** via the Incident Report process.
- Report equipment defects, environmental hazards, and aggressive behaviour the same day.

---

## 5. Medication & Clinical Boundaries

| Activity | Allowed when | Not allowed |
|----------|-------------|-------------|
| Medication prompting | Care plan authorises + training current | Changing doses, crushing tablets unless plan says so |
| OTC medicines | Explicit written authorisation in care plan | Recommending or supplying OTC drugs |
| Clinical observations | As trained and documented in plan | Diagnosing or advising on treatment |
| Emergency response | First aid within training; call 112/999 | Delaying emergency calls to "wait and see" |

Escalate health concerns through the office / GP / HSE pathway documented for each client.

---

## 6. Uniform, ID & Appearance

- Company polo or tunic, closed non-slip shoes, visible photo ID badge on all visits.
- Short, clean nails; minimal jewellery (plain wedding band, small stud earrings).
- Long hair tied back during personal care; facial piercings covered if policy requires.
- Personal hygiene standards must be high; strong perfume avoided (some clients are sensitive).

---

## 7. Data Protection & IT Use

- Client data may only be accessed for work purposes on approved devices.
- Do not screenshot or export client lists to personal storage.
- Report lost phones, suspected phishing, or data breaches to HR within **2 hours**.
- Shared passwords are prohibited.

---

## 8. Disciplinary Framework

| Stage | Typical triggers | Outcome |
|-------|-----------------|---------|
| Informal coaching | Minor first issues (late notes, minor lateness) | Documented conversation |
| Written warning | Repeated minor breaches or moderate single breach | Improvement plan |
| Final written warning | Serious or repeated breach | Last chance before dismissal |
| Summary dismissal | Theft, abuse, fraud, serious safeguarding failure, gross misconduct | Immediate termination |

Employees may be accompanied by a colleague or union representative at formal disciplinary meetings where applicable.

## 10. Whistleblowing & Raising Concerns

Employees who raise genuine concerns about unsafe practice, fraud, or safeguarding failures in good faith are protected from retaliation. Report via:

- Line manager or on-call supervisor
- HR confidential email: hr@homecarescheduler.example
- External safeguarding / regulatory channels where appropriate

Do not delay reporting because you lack full evidence. Document what you observed objectively.

---

## 11. Alcohol, Drugs & Fitness for Work

| Substance / condition | Policy |
|-----------------------|--------|
| Alcohol on duty | Zero tolerance during visits or travel between clients |
| Prescription drugs affecting alertness | Declare to HR; medical clearance may be required |
| Illegal drugs | Gross misconduct |
| Fatigue | Report if unfit; do not drive if impaired |

---

## 12. Client Property & Financial Transactions

- Do not handle client cash except as explicitly authorised (e.g. small grocery float with receipt).
- Receipt all purchases; leave change and receipt in agreed place.
- Report missing money or valuables immediately; do not investigate alone.
- Never store client bank cards or PIN details on personal devices.

---

## 13. Vehicle & Travel (Caregivers Using Own Car)

- Valid driving licence and business-use insurance required.
- Mileage claims per company schedule; journeys must be roster-related.
- No unauthorised passengers during client visits.
- Report accidents involving company business immediately.
